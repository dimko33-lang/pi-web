# v0.12.0 — Remember the person, not the scroll

You and Nikita are working through a geometry problem in a Telegram group. You leave; the room spends the next thousand messages talking about Venice. When you come back and ask “Where were we?”, which conversation should she remember? In this release you can choose yours, the room’s, or both. Nikita assembles that context **before the model request**. She does not send the whole room and then ask the model to ignore it.

## Three answers to “what should Nikita remember?”

- **Person** — your own exchanges with Nikita **in this group**, including her replies to you. A Telegram user ID and the reply link identify the exchange; a display name, a topic or the fact that you pressed Reply does not. Someone else using your nickname does not inherit your conversation.
- **Group** — Nikita’s exchanges with **other** participants here. Enable it when the discussion is shared, or leave it paused when another person’s conversation would get in the way.
- **BG** — the recent group feed, including people talking to each other without addressing Nikita. BG can include a dialogue post as part of that feed; overlapping selections include the post only once.

Open the group in `/profiles`. Each label is an independent switch. Click **Person**, enter **100** to its right, leave **Group** and **BG** dim, then **Save**. That is up to 100 **individual messages** in your dialogue, not 100 pairs and not your share of the last 100 room posts. Click **Person** again and both the word and `100` dim. The number survives Save and reload; the source contributes nothing until resumed. Typing a positive count enables a window; typing zero pauses it. Each window accepts 0–500. There is no master switch or slider. With all three paused, there is no automatic conversation history. An explicit same-chat Telegram reply can still quote that one message, not its author’s whole history.

**What we tested:** after 1,000 intervening synthetic exchanges with a different participant, the actual JSON received by a local test provider contained the intended 100 messages and none of the unrelated dialogue. All eight on/off combinations were checked, including three paused switches with nonzero counts. No live conversation was needed for the test.

The index remembers confirmed reply IDs, up to 500 **per participant**. Nikita re-reads the available text in the same Telegram group. This is not a second archive of message bodies: deleted or inaccessible exchanges are not reconstructed. Older, pre-index history has a bounded first lookup (up to 48 recent outgoing replies), not a complete import. Availability, the New Chat boundary and a byte budget can shorten a window. Old Chat reopens retained history; neither action erases the saved counts. Existing installations and saved profiles are not silently migrated.

## One private chat, one profile

Individual DMs, introduced in v0.11.0, are already part of Nikita. The owner can explicitly enroll a person and set their own base instructions, character, custom prompt, response style, model, thinking level and tools switch. Each guest has a separate private conversation; their memory control is **Dialogue**, not shared group history. Enabling tools does not grant owner-only permissions.

**Text** and **Voice** choose the reply format per profile. With the optional Gemini speech service connected, a short answer can arrive as a native Telegram voice note. Select both to answer a new microphone note with voice and a typed message with text. The profile’s configured speech path in this release uses the **Gacrux** preset. Choosing Voice does **not** select a different speaker, train on an uploaded clip or copy an actor by name.

## A salon where you can read the whole answer

In opt-in showcase groups, ordinary prose stays open up to Telegram’s message limit. LaTeX and long replies use a native Telegram article rather than a folded quotation. Interactive content can use a verified individual Notes link when a publisher is available; without one, the full Markdown can stay in the chat. Showcase formatting does not give Nikita permission to speak in a group.

See [PI-WEB Chat](https://t.me/PiWebChat) and [Salon Niki](https://t.me/SalonNikiChat). The [installation card](https://dimko33.xyz/) points new installs to a versioned archive with a SHA-256 checksum. Rerunning the installer is not a general upgrade command.

## Where Nikita is headed

**The Library of Babel.** Imagine asking: *“I proposed testing the idea with two clocks. Where did I describe the experiment, and why did I later change it?”* The idea is a **personal card catalogue of ideas**, not a dossier on the room. A participant would choose which shared groups may contribute their own posts. Nikita would look them up in a server-side index, put the original passages side by side and link back to the sources, rather than trawling Telegram on every question. The design calls for explicit enrollment, source-chat access checks, and a way to remove contributions. No automatic cross-group catalogue is enabled in v0.12.0.

A second goal is a **voice for this DM**: one person might prefer a quiet male baritone while another keeps Gacrux. Start with a named preset or a description; explore a short, authorized voice sample (target: about 15 seconds) on providers that support it. A film character can be a reference for register, pace or delivery, not a guarantee of that actor’s exact voice. The choice would affect spoken replies in that DM only.

**Telegram video notes, in character.** The idea is not to turn every answer into a video. You would choose which formats are welcome in your DM. Within that choice, Nikita could write a line, let you hear the pause before a punchline, or send a round Telegram video note when a raised eyebrow says more than another sentence. The character you chose and the conversation you are having would guide that decision.

**A familiar face from one note to the next.** You could describe the character or supply a portrait you have permission to use as an avatar. It would remain the visual reference for this DM until you replace or remove it. Appearance, clothing, setting, era and manner would be yours to choose or leave to Nikita: an 18th-century salon host might close her fan before answering, while a modern astronomer might turn from a chalkboard. Neither choice would change anyone else’s character.

**Further out: a live conversation.** A link in the Telegram DM could open a separate Zoom meeting where you could speak with the same character in real time. That is a proposed handoff from chat to a meeting, not a native Telegram video call or an integration that ships today.

The cross-group catalogue, custom voice, video notes and live meetings are plans, not features of v0.12.0.
